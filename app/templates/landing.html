Features

🧩

Widget Dashboard

Mix live widgets — clock, news, stocks, crypto, and weather — on a flexible 12-column grid.

➕

Add & Arrange

Drop in widgets from the picker and resize them from their edges to fit your screen.

⚙️

Per-Widget Settings

Tune timezone, news categories, stock symbols, crypto coins, weather location, and refresh rates.

💾

Saves As You Go

Settings and layout persist to your browser's IndexedDB the moment they change — survive any refresh.

🎛️

Built-in Layouts

Apply Simple, Essentials, or the Full Showcase preset in one click, then customize from there.

📤

Import / Export

Download your whole dashboard as JSON to migrate machines, or upload a saved layout.

🔄

News Slideshow

Prefer a single focus? The V1 view auto-rotates headlines with smooth fade transitions.

🌡️

Clock & Weather

A live clock in your chosen timezone plus current weather for any location you set.

📱

Responsive Layout

The dashboard adapts for smaller screens so it stays usable on tablets and phones.

How It Works

1

Add Widgets

Open the picker and drop in the widgets you want — clock, news, stocks, crypto, weather.

2

Arrange & Tune

Resize widgets to fit, open each one's settings to tailor its data, and pick a built-in layout.

3

It Just Stays

Everything saves to your browser automatically, so a refresh or return visit restores it all.
