Features

🔄

Auto-Rotating Slideshow

Articles cycle automatically with smooth fade transitions and a live progress bar at the top.

📂

Multiple Categories

Pick from World, Technology, Business, Science, and Sports. Articles interleave round-robin across feeds.

➕

Custom RSS Feeds

Add and remove as many custom feed URLs as you like. They group separately from built-in topics.

💾

Settings Persistence

Selected topics, custom feeds, and rotation speed save to your browser's IndexedDB — no backend needed.

🏃

Adjustable Speed

Set rotation speed from 3 to 60 seconds per article to match your reading pace.

📤

Import / Export

Download all settings as JSON to migrate machines, or import a previously saved file.

⌨️

Keyboard Support

Use ← / → to navigate articles, Space to pause/resume rotation, and more.

📱

Responsive Layout

The dashboard adapts for smaller screens, switching from a two-column card to a single column.

🌡️

Clock & Weather

Top bar shows a live clock in your chosen timezone and current weather for a selected location.

How It Works

1

Select Categories

Tick built-in news topics or add custom RSS feed URLs through the settings panel.

2

Watch Feeds Cycle

Articles interleave round-robin across all active feeds with smooth fade transitions.

3

Navigate & Customize

Use footer controls, keyboard shortcuts, or adjust speed and location at any time.
